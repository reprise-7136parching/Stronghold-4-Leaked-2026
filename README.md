## 🕹️ About

Stronghold 4 is a medieval real-time strategy and castle-building game set in England, built around managing settlements, raising armies, and surviving siege warfare. This repository contains an unofficial leaked pre-release build that surfaced ahead of its planned 2026 release, with raw campaign and castle-simulation content still in development.

For strategy players who want medieval castle management, real-time warfare, and an unpolished early-build atmosphere.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🏰 **Medieval Castle Building** — Design and expand fortified settlements with walls, defenses, and working infrastructure.
- 🌾 **Castle Economy** — Manage food, resources, workers, popularity, and the daily life of your domain.
- ⚔️ **Real-Time Sieges** — Command armies through brutal medieval battles and fortified assaults.
- 🌦️ **Battlefield Conditions** — Adapt your strategy to weather, terrain, and changing battlefield pressure.
- 📜 **Cinematic Campaign** — Follow a story-driven medieval campaign rooted in the series' English setting.
- 🛡️ **Skirmish Warfare** — Test castle layouts, armies, and siege tactics in replayable skirmish battles.
- 🧱 **Castle Customization** — Shape your stronghold with a broad selection of castle pieces and visual options.
- 🧪 **Raw Pre-Release Build** — Expect unfinished edges, experimental balance, and content that was never meant to be public yet.

 **A:** Early development builds sometimes slip out through internal test channels, distribution mishaps, or staging environments. The exact chain of events is unclear, but the build is now in the wild and preserved here for those who want to see the game in its raw, unfinished state.

 **A:** No. This is a partial, in-development snapshot. Large portions of the game may be unfinished, cut content might still be present, and the final polish, balance, and optimization are missing. Think of it as a time capsule of the project at an early stage.

 **Q: Is it safe to download and play?**
 **A:** Yes — the build is a standalone Windows executable that requires no installation, online authentication, or registry changes. It runs entirely from its own folder and can be removed by simply deleting it.

 **Q: Why is the archive password-protected?**
 **A:** Password protection slows down automated scanning bots and reduces the chance of mass takedown requests. It keeps the build accessible to those who are actually looking for it. The password is provided in the release notes.

 **Q: Will this affect my official game install or saves?**
 **A:** No. This build is completely self-contained. It does not interact with your existing game files, save data, cloud sync, or platform libraries. It runs independently and leaves no traces behind after deletion.
